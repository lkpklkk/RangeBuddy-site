Getting started

  1. 1

    Set up your iPhone

    Put it on a tripod or stand so your whole body and the ball are in frame. Use the level to straighten it. For the tracer, set it behind you, looking down the target line.

  2. 2

    Tap Start practice

    RangeBuddy records and clips each swing on its own. You don’t need to touch the phone between shots.

  3. 3

    Turn on Tracer if you like

    Switch on Tracer on the capture screen to draw each shot’s flight. A short setup shows how to frame it. It works at 60 FPS and uses more battery.

  4. 4

    Review your session

    Watch each swing with its traced shot, draw on it, and share or save the ones you like.

Questions

Where are my videos saved?

In RangeBuddy on your iPhone, grouped by practice session. To keep a copy somewhere else, open a swing and tap Share, or save it to Photos.

Why wasn’t a swing saved?

RangeBuddy skips practice swings: if the ball is still sitting there after you swing, the swing isn’t saved. For real shots, make sure your whole body and the ball stay in frame, and that the phone doesn’t move during the session.

If a real swing wasn’t kept, tap Missed a swing? on the capture screen to save it from the last 15 seconds.

Why is there no trace on a shot?

Check that Tracer is on, on the capture screen. The tracer needs to see the ball leave: keep the ball in frame with space above it, clear other balls and tees off the mat, and avoid filming straight into the sun. You can still review and share the swing without a trace.

Why can’t I turn on Tracer?

Tap the switch to see why. Tracer works only at 60 FPS and with the phone behind you, looking down the target line. It also turns off while the phone is too hot: swings still record, and it comes back when the phone cools.

Does Tracer use more battery?

Yes. Switch Tracer off any time to save battery; swings still record and clip as usual.

What do the two tracer colours mean?

The tracer colour (orange by default) is where RangeBuddy saw the ball.

Yellow is the projected rest of the flight, estimated from what it saw.

I’m left-handed

Go to Settings › Golfer and choose Left-handed.

Can I change the tracer or the app’s colours?

Choose the tracer’s look and colour in Settings › Appearance › Tracer style, or from any swing’s Tracer menu. The app’s colour theme, Home Screen icon and share watermark are in Settings › Appearance too.

RangeBuddy is using a lot of storage

Swing videos take space. Delete swings or sessions you no longer need, after saving any you want to keep to Photos.

Do I need an account?

No. RangeBuddy is free to download and works without an account. Your swings stay on your iPhone. See Privacy.

What is RangeBuddy Plus+?

An optional one-time purchase that unlocks more tracer styles, watermarks and colour themes. There’s no subscription. Recording, swing detection and ball tracing are free.

I already bought Plus+

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, go to Settings › RangeBuddy Plus+ and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete swings or sessions in the app, or delete RangeBuddy from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.
