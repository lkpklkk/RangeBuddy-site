Privacy

Your swings stay on your iPhone.

Last updated 15 November 2026

At a glance

  • Data collectedNone

  • Where swings are keptYour iPhone

RangeBuddy has no servers. We, the developer, never receive your videos, sessions or settings.

What stays on your iPhone

RangeBuddy films your practice, finds each swing, traces the ball and saves the result inside the app. All of this happens on your iPhone. Swing detection and ball tracing run on the device, not in the cloud.

Stored in the app on your iPhone:

  • practice sessions and swing videos
  • ball traces, drawings and notes you add
  • your settings, such as tracer style, theme and handedness

What RangeBuddy asks to use

  • Camera

    Films your practice so RangeBuddy can find each swing and trace the ball.

  • Microphone

    Records the sound of impact with your swing videos.

  • Motion

    Helps you level the camera before you start recording.

  • Add to Photos

    Saves the videos and snapshots you choose to save. RangeBuddy can add to your library but can’t see what’s in it.

You can change any of these in Settings › RangeBuddy on your iPhone.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you tap Share or Save. When you share, the video or snapshot goes wherever you send it, and that app’s own privacy policy applies.

Shared videos carry a RangeBuddy badge with a QR code that links to RangeBuddy. The badge contains no information about you.

Purchases and crash reports

RangeBuddy contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Any purchase in RangeBuddy is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Delete swings or sessions in the app at any time. Deleting RangeBuddy from your iPhone removes everything it stored.

If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone’s backups can include RangeBuddy’s data, as with other apps. Apple manages those backups.

Children and changes

RangeBuddy collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
