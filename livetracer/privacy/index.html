Privacy

Your shots stay on your iPhone.

Last updated 19 November 2026

At a glance

  • Data collectedNone
  • AccountNot needed

  • Where shots are keptYour iPhone

LiveTracer has no servers. We, the developer, never receive your videos, locations or settings.

What stays on your iPhone

LiveTracer films your shot, follows the ball and draws its flight. All of this happens on your iPhone. Ball tracking runs on the device, not in the cloud.

Stored in the app on your iPhone:

  • your shot videos and their traces
  • where each day’s shots were taken, if you allow location
  • your settings, such as whether you’ve seen the walkthrough

What LiveTracer asks to use

  • Camera

    Films your shot so LiveTracer can follow the ball and draw its flight.

  • Microphone

    Records sound with your traced videos.

  • Motion

    Reads how the phone moves while you film, so the trace stays on the ball when your hands aren’t perfectly still.

  • Location, while using the app

    Labels each day’s shots with the nearby course or town. Optional; without it, shots just have no place name.

  • Add to Photos

    Saves the videos and snapshots you choose to save. LiveTracer can add to your library but can’t see what’s in it.

You can change any of these in Settings › LiveTracer on your iPhone.

Location and Apple Maps

If you allow location, LiveTracer saves where each shot was taken with the shot, on your iPhone. To turn that into a name, it asks Apple Maps for the nearest golf course or town. That lookup goes to Apple, not to us, and Apple’s privacy policy applies to it.

The place name is kept on your iPhone so the same spot isn’t looked up twice. LiveTracer never tracks your location in the background.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you tap Share or Save. When you share, the video or snapshot goes wherever you send it, and that app’s own privacy policy applies.

Shared videos can carry a small “LiveTracer” watermark. It contains no information about you.

Crash reports

LiveTracer contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and has no in-app purchases.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Delete shots in the app at any time. Deleting LiveTracer from your iPhone removes everything it stored, including saved locations.

If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone’s backups can include LiveTracer’s data, as with other apps. Apple manages those backups.

Children and changes

LiveTracer collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
