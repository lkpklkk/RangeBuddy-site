Getting started

  1. 1

    Frame the shot

    Imagine you are looking out for your friend's ball, stand behind, try to keep the phone level with the ground using the built in leveler.

  2. 2

    Tap record

    Tap the shutter before the golfer sets up. The top corner then shows three steps: 1 Step into frame, 2 Take address, 3 Find the ball.

  3. 3

    Wait for Ready

    When all three are ticked, the corner reads Ready and the box around the ball turns green. If the box is on the wrong spot, double-tap the ball.

  4. 4

    Hit the shot

    The trace follows the ball as it flies. If the ball gets small, slide the zoom or tap 2. When the shot ends, the badge by the ball says Traced, or why it lost the ball.

  5. 5

    Watch it back

    Your shots are grouped by day. Save or share the ones you like, or turn on Settings › Save shots to Photos to keep every shot in Photos too.

The first time you open the camera, a short walkthrough shows each step. To see it again, go to Settings › Reset hints.

Questions

The corner is stuck on a step

Step into frame: get the golfer’s whole body in the picture. Take address: the golfer stands still over the ball for a moment. Find the ball: if LiveTracer doesn’t find it, double-tap the ball.

The box isn’t on the ball

Double-tap the ball to move the box there. Tap reset to let LiveTracer search on its own again.

Why is there no trace on a shot?

The tracer needs to see the ball leave. Wait for Ready and the green box before the swing, keep the ball in frame, and avoid filming straight into the sun. Zooming in helps when the ball is far away.

If the badge says Camera lost, the picture moved too fast or showed too little background to follow. Move the phone smoothly and keep some ground or trees in view.

What do the two tracer colours mean?

The tracer colour is where LiveTracer saw the ball.

Yellow is the projected rest of the flight, estimated from what it saw.

Why does LiveTracer ask for my location?

To label each day’s shots with the course or town you were at. It’s optional: say no and your shots simply have no place name. See Privacy.

Where are my videos saved?

In LiveTracer on your iPhone, grouped by day. To keep a copy somewhere else, share a shot or save it to Photos.

Can every shot go to Photos?

Yes. Turn on Settings › Save shots to Photos. Each shot is saved with its tracer as soon as it’s ready, and stays in LiveTracer too.

LiveTracer is using a lot of storage

Videos take space. Delete shots you no longer need, after saving any you want to keep to Photos.

How do I delete my data?

Delete shots in the app, or delete LiveTracer from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.
